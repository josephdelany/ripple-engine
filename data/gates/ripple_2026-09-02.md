## Ruling 2 — JODI-Oil: is "freely available" a licence?

Verified: the download page, the format, the columns, the coverage. The bulk `world_ext.zip`
(16 MB) is **not CSV** — it is Beyond 20/20 `.ivt`, unreadable without their browser. The
per-year CSVs are real and clean (SDMX long format: REF_AREA, TIME_PERIOD, ENERGY_PRODUCT,
FLOW_BREAKDOWN, UNIT_MEASURE, OBS_VALUE; 118 reporting countries in 2002, 96 in the partial
2026 file; monthly production, refinery intake and output, stocks, imports, exports, 2002 →).

The blocker is not technical. **No licence or terms-of-use page exists on the site.** The only
statement is "The JODI-Oil World Database is freely available and is updated on or around the
20th of each month", plus a `© Copyright JODI 2026` footer. Our rule is that an unread licence
means refresh-only at best, and I did not load it at all rather than guess.

Ruling: (a) "freely available" is enough → I write the per-year CSV loader and mark it
refresh-only, not seeded; (b) not enough → JODI stays a gap line and monthly country-level
production stays unavailable to the chain study. This is the single largest missing physical
dataset in the register.

## Ruling 3 — IMF PortWatch terms

Seven chokepoints are loaded and working (Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Suez, Cape of Good Hope,
Malacca, Panama, Bosporus; 2019-01-01 → 2026-08-30, 2,799 days each). The ArcGIS item points
its licence at `imf.org/external/terms.htm`, which returned **HTTP 403** to every attempt
(Cloudflare challenge), and the Wayback fallback was rate-limited. So the terms text has never
been read by anyone here.

Consequence today: PortWatch data is refresh-only and never committed, so a fresh clone cannot
reproduce those series offline. Ruling: (a) you open that page in your own browser and paste
what it says, and if it permits redistribution I seed them; or (b) it stays refresh-only
permanently, which is survivable — the transit nodes are 2019→ and INSUFFICIENT by
construction anyway.

## Two things you should not have to ask about, stated plainly

**The headline result is mostly nulls, and the one number I would put weight on is a
disconfirmation.** E-8 registered the expectation that at least one tightening class beats
its VIX-and-GPR-matched placebo at Brent, h=5. None does. Twenty-one of 477 cells transmit
against 1–24 expected if nothing transmits at all. The honest reading of this run is that the
chain study has not yet found transmission that survives its own controls, and the summary
says so in those words.

**The ICE Brent positioning series you asked for does not exist in the CFTC files.** Every
Brent-named contract there is NYMEX or ICE Futures Energy Div; ICE Futures Europe Brent — the
London benchmark — is absent under any name. What I loaded is NYMEX "Brent Last Day" (code
06765T, 2011-10-18 →), labelled PROXY in the series name, the register and the handoff. If you
need real ICE Brent positioning it is a different (probably paid) source, and that is a gap.
