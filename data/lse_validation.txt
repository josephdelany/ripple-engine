    "That licence [MIT] extends to the library alone and confers no rights in the

     own research, trading and model training, including for commercial purposes.
     It may not be redistributed, resold, or otherwise made available to third
     parties, sourced from LSE."

  READING:
   * Research use: EXPLICITLY PERMITTED (satisfies the registration gate).
   * Redistribution / resale / making-available-to-third-parties: PROHIBITED.

  >>> DECISIVE CONSTRAINT FOR THIS REPO <<<
  This repository commits data artifacts (samples/receipts) as source-of-truth and
  is a shareable research object. Committing LSE-sourced data would be
  REDISTRIBUTION -- forbidden by the terms. Therefore LSE data can NEVER be
  committed here and can NEVER be a canonical, checked-in series. FRED/EIA are
  public-domain and committable; that asymmetry alone keeps them canonical.

    read  client.history(symbol=..., dataset=..., timeframe="1d"|"tick"|..., ...)
