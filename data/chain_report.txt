petchem/fertilizer -> food? Each hop measured on its OWN cadence (daily vs monthly, never mixed).
  beta = OLS slope of downstream log-return on upstream log-return at the best lag (elasticity).
  lag  = the lag maximising return correlation (DAYS for daily hops, MONTHS for monthly).
  corr = correlation at that lag; strength is a descriptive band (|corr|>=.5 strong, >=.3

CAUTION: co-movement, not causation; the lag is the best-FITTING descriptive lag, not a tested
claim. A CONFIRMATORY hop-transmission claim needs a REGISTERED amendment BEFORE results.
