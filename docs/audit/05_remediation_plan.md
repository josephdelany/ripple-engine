## Why R0a and R1 are the whole job

Before them, the paper claims:

> Formalised historical analogy contains no out-of-sample predictive information about geopolitical
> escalation and oil prices.

The design cannot support that. It tested within-class reranking on a mostly-empty state vector,
against a country-violence target and a raw-return target.

After them, the paper claims:

> Within-class reranking on a mostly macro-financial state vector does not beat within-class pooling
> for a country-violence target, and does not beat the unconditional distribution of raw 20-day
> returns for price. The structural version could not be tested: 84% of events carry no knowable
> structural state, 26% of reads admit no selection, and the physical layer is 6% recoverable from
> public sources.

**Narrower by a wide margin, and unlike the first version, supported by the evidence.**

---

## What NOT to do

- **Do not re-run the walk to chase a better number.** Every route to more power is measured shut:
  backward expansion buys zero scored reads, escalation labels die at 2014, and training was tested
  at n_eff ≈ 2,000 and did not beat fixed constants.
- **Do not fix C1 or C2.** Rebuilding the state vector with fundamentals or restricting to one
  regime is a new project, not a revision.
- **Do not delete the withdrawn claims.** Corrections of record, in the pattern the project already
  uses for its four retractions.
