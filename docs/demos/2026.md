Everyone missed by roughly the same absolute amount, because nothing in the record had
produced a +47% twenty-day move from a chokepoint event. But the engine did not merely

a wide climatological spread (25 atoms) down to ten near-identical seizures, and that
narrowing is what cost it: p90 +2.21 against climatology's wider tail.

A declared closure is not a seizure. This is the "no adequate precedent" case the engine
is built to admit, and the read shows it did not admit it: similarity over a market-only
state vector found neighbours, and the neighbours were the wrong kind.
